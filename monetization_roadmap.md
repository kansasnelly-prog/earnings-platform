# TikTok6 Monetization Roadmap

## 1. Revenue Streams
- **Launch**: Coin purchases (packages), Gifts.
- **Future**: Ads (in-feed), Creator Marketplace (commission), Subscriptions (premium content), E-commerce/Affiliate (integrated checkout).

## 2. Economics
- **Coin Purchases**: High-margin, fixed-price packages.
- **Gifts**: Users buy coins, spend on virtual goods; platform takes 50% revenue cut.
- **Creator Payout**: Payout based on earned "Diamonds" (value from gifts), converted at a fixed rate.
- **Ads/Marketplace/Subscription/E-commerce**: Integrated later to diversify revenue.

## 3. Growth Roadmap
- **1-Year**: Focus on Launch revenue (Coins/Gifts), establishing payout compliance.
- **3-Year**: Expand to Ads/Creator Marketplace/Subscriptions.
- **5-Year**: Full E-commerce integration and global scale.

## 4. Compliance & Scaling
- **Compliance**: Tax (VAT/sales tax), KYC/AML for payouts, T&Cs.
- **Scaling**: Geographic sharding, localized payout support, localized currency support.
